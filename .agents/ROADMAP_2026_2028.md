# Oplyra Product Roadmap (2026 - 2028)

This roadmap outlines the planned evolution of Oplyra from its current MVP state to an intelligent, automated marketing operating system.

---

## Roadmap Phases

### Phase 1: Current MVP Stabilization
- **Focus**: Stabilize the application core. 
- **Features**: Authentication, Client CRUD, Campaign CRUD, local SEO analyzer, `AIGateway` model routing with Redis and DB response caching, local PDF exports.
- **Priority**: High | **Difficulty**: Low | **Business Value**: Core Foundation | **Time**: Completed.

### Phase 2: Workflow Optimization & Today View
- **Focus**: Make the "Today's Work" homepage fully interactive and contextual.
- **Features**:
  - Direct integration of active campaign tasks on the Today view.
  - Quick action widgets: One-click "Extend SEO Article Draft" or "Re-format Ad Copy to Meta Specifications."
  - Calendar integrations to show campaign deadlines.
- **Priority**: High | **Difficulty**: Medium | **Business Value**: High | **Time**: 1 - 2 months.

### Phase 3: Campaign Intelligence & Analytics
- **Focus**: Ingest campaign metrics to drive task prioritization.
- **Features**:
  - Integrate CSV uploads of Facebook and Google Ads performance metrics.
  - AI-assisted analysis of conversion drops to suggest ad copy refreshes.
  - Automatically create tasks on the Today view if ad budgets are overspent.
- **Priority**: High | **Difficulty**: Medium | **Business Value**: High | **Time**: 2 - 3 months.

### Phase 4: Automated Integrations (Meta, Google, GA4)
- **Focus**: Direct API synchronization to remove manual data exporting/importing.
- **Features**:
  - Connect Facebook Ads API to read campaign delivery status.
  - Connect Google Search Console API for real-time SEO position tracking.
  - OAuth setup under Settings for safe credential management.
- **Priority**: Medium | **Difficulty**: High | **Business Value**: High | **Time**: 3 - 4 months.

### Phase 5: Background AI Agents (Automated Audits)
- **Focus**: Transform Oplyra from a reactive tool to a proactive assistant.
- **Features**:
  - Nightly cron jobs that check campaign statuses and look for conversion drops.
  - Pre-generate suggested ad copy fixes and queue them as drafts before the marketer logs in.
- **Priority**: Medium | **Difficulty**: High | **Business Value**: High | **Time**: 3 months.

### Phase 6: Core Brand Kits & Assets DAM
- **Focus**: Consolidating multi-client assets.
- **Features**:
  - Unified Asset Folder management inside Campaigns.
  - Multi-version assets history (`ContentVersion` database entries).
  - Client-shareable preview links for direct review approvals.
- **Priority**: Low | **Difficulty**: Medium | **Business Value**: Medium | **Time**: 2 months.
